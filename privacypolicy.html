Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 9, 2026

Introduction

LumeMax ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application LumeMax (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Photos and Images

When you use our beauty analysis feature, you may provide photos of yourself. These photos are:

  • Processed by our AI service to generate your personalized beauty analysis
  • NOT stored on our servers after analysis is complete
  • Processed securely using encrypted connections
  • Never shared with third parties for advertising or other purposes

Profile Information

You may voluntarily provide:

  • Your name (optional)
  • Skin type preferences
  • Skin concerns
  • Age range (optional)

This information is stored locally on your device and used solely to personalize your experience.

Analysis Results

Your beauty analysis results, including:

  • Style DNA and color season
  • Skincare recommendations
  • Product suggestions
  • Routine tracking data

These are stored locally on your device.

Usage Data

We may automatically collect:

  • App usage statistics (anonymized)
  • Device type and operating system version
  • Crash reports and performance data

This data is collected anonymously and cannot identify you personally.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide personalized beauty analysis and recommendations
  • Save your preferences and routine history locally
  • Improve our AI analysis accuracy
  • Provide customer support
  • Send routine reminders (only if you enable notifications)
  • Process subscription payments through Apple

Third-Party Services

Google Gemini AI

We use Google's Gemini AI service to analyze photos and generate beauty insights. When you submit a photo:

  • The image is sent securely to Google's API
  • Google processes the image to generate analysis
  • Google's use of this data is governed by their privacy policy
  • We do not store your photos after analysis

Apple Services

We use Apple's services for:

  • In-App Purchases and subscription management
  • Push notifications (if enabled)
  • App analytics (anonymized)

Data Storage and Security

Your data security is important to us:

  • Profile data and analysis results are stored locally on your device
  • Photos are transmitted using secure encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • We do not maintain a database of user photos
  • Subscription data is managed securely by Apple
  • We implement industry-standard security measures

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access your data stored locally in the app
  • Delete your profile and analysis history at any time
  • Opt out of notifications
  • Request information about data we may have collected
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data

To delete your data, go to Profile settings or simply uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all locally stored data from your device.

Children's Privacy

LumeMax is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Data Retention

  • Photos: Not retained after analysis is complete
  • Local profile data: Retained until you delete it or uninstall the app
  • Subscription data: Managed by Apple according to their retention policies
  • Anonymized analytics: May be retained to improve our services

International Users

If you are accessing the App from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States where our servers are located and our central database is operated.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
